## 2026-11-07

### Observation 1: Activation questions override a stated distill

**Status:** OPEN
**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Session context:** User invoked an architecture skill on a finished brief and said every decision was final, distill only cross-unit conflicts, and ask only about gaps.
**Skill:** bmad-architecture
**Type:** open-source
**Phase/Area:** On Activation, steps 5 and 6

**Issue:** The activation sequence always offers a coaching-or-fast choice and asks whether the spine is the only deliverable before drafting. The user had already named the deliverable and forbidden re-opening settled decisions. Following those two questions would have re-litigated the brief.

**Suggested improvement:** In On Activation, if the caller has already named the deliverable and marked the input's decisions as final, skip the mode offer and the deliverable question. Ask only about gaps that would let two downstream units diverge.

**Principle:** When the caller has already fixed the output and the settled decisions, an activation menu that reopens those choices is friction, not elicitation.
